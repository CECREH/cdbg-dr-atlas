# Source document catalog

The [document catalog](documents.html) connects selected atlas records to the public source locations, PDF page references and source versions recorded in the atlas evidence. Its index describes 1,718 documents and 62,269 dataset-location citations. These citations are references, not independent financial observations. Coverage is limited to the listed inputs; the catalog does not establish that every source, quarter, jurisdiction or record has been found.

`data/document-catalog.json` contains the searchable index and a citation preview. The 1,467 files in `data/document-citations/` contain complete linked-record references for entries with citations. `data/document-inputs.json` records the 42 input files and their pinned hashes from [atlas commit 4d29babaecb2f7d0875b0845abf1e2ab5c132095](https://github.com/CECREH/cdbg-dr-atlas/tree/4d29babaecb2f7d0875b0845abf1e2ab5c132095); nine listed dataset groups were not processed for this index. Page numbers are PDF page positions, which can differ from printed numbering. A record-to-source reference is not an independent review of the financial interpretation.

The catalog offers links to non-blocked source locations and downloadable metadata. It contains **no saved original documents** or approved redacted copies. Receipts report 1,267 originals in a separate research archive, but those files are not supplied or reverified here and the receipts do not authorize republication. A report's HUD review status is distinct from privacy or redistribution review. Source links can change or become unavailable, and 216 blocked entries have no source-opening action. Do not interpret a missing or blocked source as a zero value.

The index preserves separate source versions and overlapping financial snapshots. Do not add cumulative quarters together, combine the national and Texas snapshots, or treat an administering government as the beneficiary location. Award clocks, denominators, allocations and beneficiary geography remain incomplete as described in the atlas. The local catalog-generation package and original research archive are not part of this site.
