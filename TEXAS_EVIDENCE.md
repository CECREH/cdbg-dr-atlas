# Texas QPR evidence supplement

Prepared October 28, 2026 from selected public HUD and Texas General Land Office (GLO) documents. This is an additional evidence view; it does not replace the atlas's national or Texas F31 snapshots or the older document catalog. All source observations are attributed to the state-administered report or its combined reporting group, not to a beneficiary city. The full research request remains incomplete.

## Coverage and grain

- **406 activity-quarter observations**: 319 from the approved 2023Q2 Harvey combined parent `P-17-TX-48-HIM1`, 10 from the approved 2016-floods 2018Q3 QPR, 64 from its approved 2022Q3 QPR, and 13 from the approved $57.8 million award's 2023Q1 QPR. Each row is one `(reporting_group, calendar_quarter, project, activity)` key. Parent activity expenditures are combined and do not become award-specific when there is an F31 key match. An original-award ID is blank for that parent.
- **84 place references**: 50 service-area-name references from the Harvey parent report and 34 project-location-name references from the 2016-floods Q20 report. Eight are Census-designated-place references and six flag outside-place or multiple-area scope. Two Harvey names and two 2016-floods names did not match exactly and have no Census ID. The other 80 use exact 2024 Census Gazetteer place-name references; this later vintage does not certify a 2022–2023 beneficiary area or legal government identity. No beneficiary allocations or weights are inferred.
- **Eight whole-award evidence rows**: three approved, award-clock milestone observations; two approved calendar-quarter observations with unresolved clocks; and three located submitted reports with no accepted milestone values. A report describes a historical period but may have been published later; publication dates are retained. Submitted, missing and unverified are not zeros.

## Award clock and denominators

Q1 is the calendar quarter containing the agreement date, which may be partial. Q4, Q12 and Q20 are not exact anniversaries. The GLO [2016 floods timeline p. 2](https://www.glo.texas.gov/sites/default/files/resources/cdr/documents/grant-administration/reports/legislative-quarterly-reports/2016-floods-allocation-timeline.pdf#page=2) gives the executed date and award ID `B-16-DL-48-0001`, yielding Q4 in 2018Q3 and Q20 in 2022Q3. Both approved QPRs report a $238,895,000 award; E / reported award uses that **same-report** figure and does not certify the initial award or an activity/city allocation. The located Q12 report is submitted, awaiting review, so this supplement leaves its outcomes blank.

The [HUD OIG report p. 5](https://www.hudoig.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/2021-FW-1001.pdf#page=5) identifies the $5.024 billion agreement and original award `B-17-DM-48-0001` as dated August 17, 2018; a [contemporaneous GLO announcement](https://www.glo.texas.gov/about-glo/press-releases/cmr-george-p-bush-commemorates-year-recovery-hurricane-harvey) says HUD signed the agreement that day. The approved 2023Q2 parent QPR separately lists cumulative E and O for B17, supporting Q20 E/O. The parent award amount combines grants and is not an acceptable B17 allocation denominator. Q4 and Q12 located reports are submitted and their milestone values remain blank.

The approved 2023Q1 QPR for `B-17-DL-48-0002` supports calendar-quarter E/O and E/reported award, but the execution date remains unresolved, so no Q20 is assigned. For `B-16-DH-48-0001` 2018Q1 the table's cumulative expenditure is $122,125.94 while the narrative reports Q1 expenditure of $241,136.70; scopes or timing may differ. Its date is also unresolved. Ratios are withheld for review; neither number is silently reconciled.

## Fields and units

`texas-evidence-v55.json` has arrays `whole_awards`, `activities` and `place_references`. Downloadable CSV files expose the same flat rows. `*_cents` fields are signed integer strings in nominal US cents, preserving negative adjustments and avoiding floating-point rounding. Blank is unavailable, not zero. `*_period_cents` is reported for the quarter; `*_to_date_cents` is cumulative in the particular report. Budget is the reported activity budget, not a certified allocation. Drawdowns are distinct from expenditures. Decimal ratios are expenditure divided by a strictly positive denominator, with missing and nonpositive denominators unavailable. In the award table, `reported_award_cents` is the header of that same QPR and is not the programmed activity budget. `publication_date` is the source index timestamp, not the end of the reported period. `source_sha256` identifies the checked bytes where available; the public source may later be revised or unavailable.

Place rows identify `geography_role`, Census name, GEOID, LSAD, functional status and vintage. `outside_city_explicit` and `multi_area_statement` flag relevant source language where assessed; blank means not assessed. `beneficiary_boundary_verified` is false and beneficiary dollars are blank. The 2024 [Texas Census Gazetteer](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2024_Gazetteer/2024_gaz_place_48.txt) is a name/identity reference, not a historical allocation boundary. The public data omit source narratives and street-level details; follow the official source page for context.

## Reconciliation and verification

An independent Poppler layout-text pass checked identifiers and 5,510 period/to-date financial cells across all 406 activity rows against the locally saved PDFs. All 84 selected place names appeared on their cited pages; 80 Census name/GEOID/type/status references matched the 2024 Gazetteer, and four names remain unresolved. The same-report whole-grant headers, key overall cells and agreement evidence were visually inspected; the activity-level check is automated, not a complete visual audit. The public package retains only reviewed structured values and official links, not original documents or raw descriptions.

The 319 displayed Harvey parent activity expenditures sum to $3,353,861,183.63, versus the parent overall $3,541,065,710.33, a $187,204,526.70 difference. Obligations also differ. The source may omit activities, so no forced reconciliation is made. Likewise, the $57.8 million report's displayed activity expenditures sum to $17,870,940.37 versus $18,079,783.09 overall. Neither an activity sum nor F31 flows are a substitute for the report's whole-grant cumulative total. A CSV download represents only its declared scope; filtered CSV exports include all matching rows across pages.

The three incoming ZIPs were integrity-checked and archived locally. One ZIP intentionally contained no original documents even though its original manifest listed 15; eight were matched by hash to copies in the other packages or subsequently acquired from official sources. Seven listed originals remain absent. Original PDFs, contact details and address narratives are not bundled with this website. Some source links may be unavailable or change. See the separate document catalog for its own coverage and blocked-source policies; three of the 12 distinct source URLs in this supplement are not indexed there.
