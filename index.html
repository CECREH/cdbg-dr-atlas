READ THE LIMITS WITH THE DATA

Readiness is more than row counts.

Keep geography, accounting,
timing and inference distinct.

01

Location needs its own evidence.

Administering-grantee identity, a responsible organization's name and a valid geography code do not locate beneficiaries. Candidate matches stay candidates. Multi-area and unresolved records remain unallocated.

Documented administrative activity scopes are distinct from beneficiary exposure. Overlapping county and city areas cannot be summed as disjoint geographic exposure.

02

One snapshot. One accounting view.

National and Texas extracts overlap. Later values may reflect completion, corrections or both; no automatic supersession rule is established. The explorer requires one snapshot and never adds the two.

Quarterly flows retain signs. Observed-window sums are not certified award-to-date totals. Disbursements remain separate from expenditures.

03

Milestones remain unavailable.

Q4, Q12 and Q20 refer to quarters relative to verified initial grant execution, under an explicit horizon convention. A reporting-period begin date, first observed row, award year or rounded grant age cannot substitute.

Ratios also require complete or reconciled opening history, same-scope dated allocation/obligation denominators and uncensored endpoints. Zero or negative denominators need explicit rules.

04

Context is not a capacity finding.

ACS estimates, flood exposure, land cover, permits and government resources have different units, vintages and missingness. Boundary-consistent city joins are required. Suppression does not establish zero staff or zero exposure.

Exploratory accounting associations do not establish city capacity, recovery effectiveness or execution-rate effects. Weak effective inference and shared-disaster dependence must be evaluated before making such claims.

Field guide

Financial record key
Source snapshot + original award + project + activity + calendar quarter. Identifiers are strings; padding is preserved.
Calendar quarter
Derived from the actual reporting-period begin date. National source labels use federal fiscal quarters. A conventional quarter-end date does not certify reporting completeness.
Money and precision
Nominal USD. Integer cents are preserved relative to the supplied CSV; this does not certify original upstream precision. Zero is reported zero; a dash or “Unavailable” is missing.
Administering geography
A government/entity identity with documented reference-area correspondence. Map points do not imply a financial allocation or historical polygon equivalence.
Reported activity scope
A period-specific documentary claim. It requires award/activity/funding-source/quarter evidence and does not automatically identify beneficiaries.
Beneficiary geography
A separately verified residence or benefit location. Unresolved beneficiary coordinates remain blank and are never replaced by administering coordinates.
Source vintage
Data period, extract label, boundary vintage and access date are different. Filename dates are not independently verified acquisition timestamps.
Missingness
Absent records, unavailable fields, suppressed values, uncensored endpoints and observed zeros are distinct. No automatic filling, clipping, population splitting or interpolation is performed.

A careful path to the requested panel.

Verify redistribution rights and audience; choose cities, cohorts and observation end date; obtain initial execution dates and dated allocation histories; certify activity geography; then reconcile context joins and milestone eligibility. Until then, use the catalog and administrative histories for evidence review.
