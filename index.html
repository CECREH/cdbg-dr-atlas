Quarter convention, denominators and snapshot policy

Q1 is the calendar reporting quarter containing the verified initial execution date; it may be partial. Q4, Q12 and Q20 end at calendar quarter ends. Annual anniversaries are not interpolated from quarterly totals.

Each ratio is cumulative expended / compatible allocated or cumulative expended / compatible obligated at the same horizon and geography. Missing and nonpositive denominators produce no ratio. Signed corrections remain. Unknown opening history, missing quarters and incomplete follow-up are flagged.

Current whole-grant awards, later amendments, activity budgets and local allocations remain separate. Public QPR cumulative evidence is an independent historical reporting vintage and is not spliced into the F31 flows. The national and Texas snapshots overlap and are never added.

    Field guide

    Obligations
    Reported signed quarterly net obligation changes; not an allocation denominator.
    Expenditures
    Reported signed quarterly net expenditure changes. Sums cover observed quarters only.
    Disbursements
    Grant and program-income disbursements remain separate from expenditures.
    Calendar vs fiscal quarter
    Calendar quarter follows the actual reporting-period start. The original fiscal label is retained in details and exports.
    Reference vintage
    2025 land-clipped Census display boundaries; government identity sources may use 2022, 2023 or 2026 references. Historical boundary equivalence is not assumed.
    Projection
    Regional Albers equal-area conic display. Lower 48: parallels 29.5° and 45.5°, central meridian 96°W. Alaska, Hawaii and territories have separate regional views. Display geometry is not used for geographic attribution, distance or area calculations.
    Statistical inference
    This is an accounting and source-exploration panel. It does not establish government capacity or causal recovery effects. Sparse local samples, shared disasters and incomplete geography constrain inference.
