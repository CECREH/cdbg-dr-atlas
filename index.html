WHAT THIS PANEL CAN SUPPORT

Definitions & data readiness.

AVAILABLE

Quarterly financial history

Original award, project, activity and calendar-quarter keys; activity type; administering and responsible identities; five signed nominal-USD financial measures. Negative corrections and observed zeros are preserved. Missing quarters are not filled with zeros.

IDENTITY ≠ FOOTPRINT

Local-government associations

Government identity can support administrative analysis by city, county/parish, township or other matched government type. It does not establish the beneficiary city or assign a state award to every subunit.

PARTIAL DOCUMENTATION

Activity geography

Exact activity/award/quarter matches to explicit city/county names in official QPR location descriptions are available in a separate layer. Submitted reports remain subject to review. Multi-area scopes overlap and have no dollar allocation weights.

UNAVAILABLE

Q4 / Q12 / Q20 execution ratios

Verified initial grant execution dates, compatible local/activity allocation histories and complete uncensored award-relative histories are missing. Projected activity starts, disaster years and first observed quarters are not substitutes. Ratios remain blank.

Field guide

Obligations
Reported signed quarterly net obligation changes; not an allocation denominator.
Expenditures
Reported signed quarterly net expenditure changes. Sums cover observed quarters only.
Disbursements
Grant and program-income disbursements remain separate from expenditures.
Calendar vs fiscal quarter
Calendar quarter follows the actual reporting-period start. The original fiscal label is retained in details and exports.
Reference vintage
2025 land-clipped Census display boundaries; government identity sources may use 2022, 2023 or 2026 references. Historical boundary equivalence is not assumed.
Projection
Regional Albers equal-area conic display. Lower 48: parallels 29.5° and 45.5°, central meridian 96°W. Alaska, Hawaii and territories have separate regional views. Display geometry is not used for geographic attribution, distance or area calculations.
Statistical inference
This is an accounting and source-exploration panel. It does not establish government capacity or causal recovery effects. Sparse local samples, shared disasters and incomplete geography constrain inference.
