How to read these facts

  • Expenditure divided by obligation is a descriptive accounting ratio. It is not recovery speed or percent completion; a ratio above one is retained. Table values are rounded to six decimal places, with ties away from zero. CSV and JSON downloads retain the full source ratio strings.
  • Q1 is the calendar quarter containing the recorded initial effective date and can be partial. Any relative quarter shown is provisional, not proof of bilateral signing or an exact anniversary. Houston's relative quarter remains unavailable.
  • Financial page numbers refer to the listed pages as a group; they are not a claim that each amount appears on each page. Source approval does not establish when the report was public.
  • Zero reported program income is not an audit certification of federal-only spending. A blank estimated program-income header is not zero. No historic allocation outcome is inferred.
  • The 34-award research frame has zero certified category dollar totals and no complete approved 20-quarter panel. Independent spatial recomputation for the separate 15-place check remains 0 of 15; this page does not change the spatial data.
