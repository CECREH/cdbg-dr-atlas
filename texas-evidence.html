AWARD-CLOCK EVIDENCE

Whole-grant observations.

For documented clocks, Q1 is the calendar quarter containing the agreement date; it may be partial. Q4, Q12 and Q20 are not exact 12-, 36- or 60-month anniversaries. Amounts are to date in nominal US dollars. A QPR’s reported award amount is the same-report denominator, not a verified initial or city allocation.

Texas’s 2016 floods award has approved Q4 and Q20 reports. The Harvey award has an approved Q20 original-award split for expenditures and obligations; its original-award allocation at Q20 remains unavailable. The $57.8 million award has a calendar-quarter report but no certified clock. For the 2015 floods report, the narrative and overall expenditure table differ; ratios are withheld pending clarification.
